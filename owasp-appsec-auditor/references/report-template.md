- Project, revision/commit, date, reviewer, authorized scope, exclusions
- Architecture, environments, assessment mode and permitted tools

## Executive summary
- Risk posture (qualified; not certification)
- Confirmed findings by severity; potential/unverified separately
- Top priorities and key business impacts

## Methodology and coverage
- OWASP Top 10 edition; API Top 10 and ASVS versions when relevant

- Coverage gaps and assumptions
